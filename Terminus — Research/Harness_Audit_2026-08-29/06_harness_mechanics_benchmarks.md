## Addendum (research agent follow-up)
- Hashline is CONTESTED: Bölük's "The Harness Problem" (16 models × 180 tasks × 3 runs, mutation tasks with known inverses) +15 pts avg, Grok Code Fast +61.6, DeepSeek V3.2 −5, output tokens −61%. Independent replication (nwyin.com edit-bench: Gemini-3-flash, Qwen3.5-397b, GPT-4.1-mini; Py/TS/Rust) found hashline **25–40 pts worse on Python** (significant whitespace), ~equal on TS, mixed on Rust; model choice dominates format.
